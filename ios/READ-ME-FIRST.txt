Calculator for Farm — iPhone app (Xcode)

What is in this folder
  ContentView.swift   the app code (paste it into your Xcode project)
  web/                the calculator itself (drag this folder into Xcode)
  AppIcon-1024.png    the app icon (black plus)

Steps (short version)
  1. Xcode > Settings > Accounts > "+" > Apple ID, sign in.
  2. File > New > Project > iOS > App > Next.
     Product Name: CalculatorForFarm   Interface: SwiftUI   Language: Swift
     Team: your Apple account.
  3. Open ContentView.swift in Xcode, delete everything, paste this folder's ContentView.swift.
  4. Drag the "web" folder into the left sidebar of Xcode.
     Tick "Copy items if needed" and your app target, then Finish.
  5. Assets > AppIcon: drag AppIcon-1024.png into the empty icon box.
  6. Target > General > Display Name: Calculator for Farm
  7. Plug in the iPhone, pick it at the top of Xcode, press the Play button.
  8. On the iPhone (first time only):
     - Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode > On (phone restarts)
     - Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > your Apple ID > Trust

Free Apple ID: the app stops opening after 7 days. Connect the iPhone and press
Play again; your saved numbers stay.
Paid Apple Developer Program (US$99/year): the app lasts a year and can be shared
with TestFlight.
